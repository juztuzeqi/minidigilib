Transkrip Resmi
📌 Cara Kerja (tulis tangan di kertas)

Attempt 1: Dengarkan audio. Tulis semua kalimat di kertas kosong.

Koreksi: Cocokkan tulisanmu dengan transkrip di bawah ini. Lingkari kesalahanmu.

Attempt 2: Tutup transkrip. Dengarkan audio lagi. Tulis ulang semua kalimat tanpa melihat kunci.

Bandingkan: Apakah Attempt 2 lebih baik dari Attempt 1?

  1. I study every evening.
  2. I studied last night.
  3. I am studying right now.
  4. She cooks on the weekends.
  5. She is cooking now.
  6. She did not cook yesterday.
  7. They play football every Saturday.
  8. They played football last Saturday.
  9. He walks to school every day.
  10. He is walking to school now.
