📌 How to do this / Cara Kerja

Attempt 1: Listen to the audio. Write all sentences on a blank paper.
(Dengarkan audio. Tulis semua kalimat di kertas kosong.)

Check: Compare your writing with the transcript below. Circle your mistakes.
(Cocokkan tulisanmu dengan transkrip di bawah. Lingkari kesalahanmu.)

Attempt 2: Hide the transcript. Listen again. Write all sentences without looking at the key.
(Tutup transkrip. Dengarkan lagi. Tulis ulang semua kalimat tanpa melihat kunci.)

Compare: Is Attempt 2 better than Attempt 1?
(Apakah Attempt 2 lebih baik dari Attempt 1?)

  2. She is my cousin, Ana.
  3. What does she do every morning?
  4. She reads a book every morning.
  5. Where does she usually read?
  6. She reads in her room.
  7. Why is she reading now?
